HOW THE AUDIO VISUALIZER WORKS
Spicetify fullscreen player + local audio bridge
===============================================

Four stages, from your speakers to the rings.


1. CAPTURE  (bridge.js, on Windows)
-----------------------------------
Spotify decodes audio natively, outside the browser engine, so nothing
running inside the extension can see the waveform. That is the entire
reason the bridge exists.

  - Watches for Spotify.exe every 2 seconds (near-zero cost while idle).
  - Finds the root Spotify process (the one whose parent is not also
    Spotify) and attaches WASAPI per-process loopback to its process tree.
  - Receives interleaved 16-bit PCM, 48 kHz stereo, of exactly what
    Spotify is playing.

Because the capture is bound to that process rather than to your output
device, game audio, Discord, browser tabs and notifications are excluded
automatically. Pausing Spotify flattens the rings even if other sound is
playing.


2. ANALYSIS  (bridge.js)
------------------------
  - Samples go into a ring buffer.
  - Every 256 samples (the "hop"), the most recent 2048 samples are taken,
    a Hann window is applied, and an FFT is run.
      window  = 2048 samples = ~43 ms, giving ~23 Hz bins
      hop     = 256 samples  = 188 analyses per second
  - Magnitudes are summed into 28 logarithmically spaced bands from
    40 Hz to 16 kHz, with a slight upward tilt so the highs are not
    buried by bass energy.
  - Each band is converted to dB and normalised against a floor and a
    ceiling that track that band's own recent range, so a quiet master
    still fills the rings and a loud one doesn't sit clipped flat.
  - Noise gate at -55 dB: near-silent bands read as zero rather than
    amplified room tone.
  - 60 ms smoothing removes FFT frame-to-frame jitter at the source.
  - Onset detection: spectral flux compared against a moving median,
    with a 110 ms minimum gap between hits.

Measured cost: about 0.105 ms per analysis cycle, 188 cycles per second,
roughly 2% of one CPU core.


3. TRANSPORT
------------
  - 28 band values, one overall energy value, and any onset flag are sent
    as JSON over a WebSocket on ws://127.0.0.1:8787, capped at about
    120 messages per second.
  - The same process serves a small HTTP lyrics proxy on that port,
    because the lyrics APIs (LRCLIB and Better Lyrics) send no CORS
    headers and a page cannot call them directly, while a local process
    can.
  - Nothing is exposed beyond 127.0.0.1.


4. RENDERING  (fullscreenPlayer.js, inside Spotify)
---------------------------------------------------
Motion model
  Every incoming value drives a spring-damper rather than a simple ease:

      v += (-k * (x - target) - 2 * zeta * sqrt(k) * v) * dt
      x += v * dt

  with k = 800 and zeta = 0.65 for the bands. Measured response: 90% in
  about 83 ms with a slight overshoot. The overshoot is what reads as
  punch; the momentum is what makes jitter physically impossible, which
  plain exponential smoothing could not do without also being laggy.

Rings
  - Three concentric rings drawn on canvas, 220 points each.
  - Each samples the spectrum around its circumference, mirrored across
    the vertical axis (bass at the top, treble at the bottom).
  - Values are centred on their mean, so lobes push outward AND pull
    inward; otherwise the whole circle just inflates.
  - Radii are expressed as fractions of the space between the artwork
    edge and the canvas edge, so they scale with whatever size the
    artwork ends up and can never clip.
  - The beat pulse scales only the artwork-hugging part of the radius.
  - Colour comes from a palette sampled out of the album cover: the image
    is drawn to a 40x40 offscreen canvas, pixels are bucketed into 12 hue
    ranges, near-black and near-white are discarded, and up to five
    colours spread around the wheel are used as a gradient.

Spectrum bars
  - 64 bars along the top and bottom edges, fed by the same band values.
  - Folded so bass sits at both outer edges and treble meets in the
    middle; a straight low-to-high ramp across a whole screen looks
    lopsided with all the movement bunched at one end.
  - Opacity and height both ride the band level.


FALLBACK
--------
If the bridge is not running, the extension drops to a slow sine
"breathing" motion after one second without packets, and retries the
connection every four seconds. Nothing breaks; it simply stops being
reactive.


WHY NOT USE SPOTIFY'S OWN DATA
------------------------------
Spotify's audio-analysis service was removed from the desktop client
(the Cosmos call returns "Resolver not found"), and the Web API
equivalents were deprecated in November 2024 with no replacement for new
applications. AcousticBrainz has beat grids but was frozen in 2022 and
skews toward mainstream catalogue. Capturing the audio locally is the
only approach with full coverage, and it yields a real spectrum rather
than precomputed metadata.


TUNING POINTS
-------------
  bridge.js
    FFT_SIZE / HOP     window length and update rate
    BANDS              band count (28)
    GATE_DB            noise gate threshold (-55)
    TAU_SPEC           source-side smoothing (0.06 s)
    Math.pow(norm, X)  dynamic range expansion (1.35)

  fullscreenPlayer.js
    SPR_K / SPR_ZETA   ring stiffness and damping (800 / 0.65)
    RINGS[]            per-ring gap, amplitude, width, alpha, speed, lobes
    CANVAS_RATIO       canvas size vs artwork; smaller = bigger artwork
    STEPS              points per ring (220) - lower this to cut CPU
